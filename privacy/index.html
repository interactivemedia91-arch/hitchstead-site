Privacy Policy

What Hitchstead keeps, where, and who can see it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026 (summary clarified; sharing through iCloud from Hitchstead 1.1, and purchase records)

In short: Hitchstead has no accounts of its own, no servers of its own, no ads and no third-party analytics. Your schedule is stored on your iPhone, and optional sharing uses your own iCloud. Apple handles payments; Apple sends us records of purchases and refunds, which we process with RevenueCat (see Purchases). We do not sell any information about you.

Your schedule

Your rotation, the changes you save, and the holidays and personal dates you add (such as birthdays) are stored on your iPhone, where the Hitchstead widget reads them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing your rotation through iCloud

If you invite someone, Hitchstead keeps a copy of your rotation (your days, the name you chose to show, and when your iPhone was last online) in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, and shares it only with the people you invite through Apple's sharing. They can see it in Hitchstead but can't change it. We, the developer, cannot read it. Your personal dates are not shared. You can stop sharing at any time in Share → Stop sharing. Apple stores this data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Following someone

If you follow someone, their rotation is copied to your iPhone from their iCloud share and refreshed when it changes. The notifications about their changes are made on your iPhone. Stopping following removes their copy from your iPhone.

Apple Calendar

If you publish, Hitchstead writes all-day events (such as "On hitch · Nights", "Travel" and "Home") into its own Hitchstead calendar on your device. If that calendar is in iCloud, Apple syncs it to your other devices under Apple's Privacy Policy, and anyone you invite to it can see those events. Removing Hitchstead doesn't delete that calendar; you can delete it in the Calendar app.

Exported files

An ICS file you export, or a picture of a month or year you share, goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Hitchstead only asks Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account owns the unlock.

To count sales and refunds, Apple sends us a record of each purchase (the product, date, country and status, for example a refund). We process these records with RevenueCat. They contain no name, email address or payment details, and the app itself doesn't send anything to RevenueCat.

Sharing and tracking

Hitchstead contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across apps or websites. The app itself sends your information to no one, apart from the iCloud sharing you start yourself; the purchase records described under Purchases come from Apple, not from the app.

Children

Hitchstead does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
