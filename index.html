Know when they're home.

Working away? Unlock Hitchstead and invite your partner or family with a private link. Following someone? Open their invite on your iPhone: their rotation stays up to date by itself, and following is free.

  • A notification when the homecoming moves, like "Mike's schedule changed: home Sun, Oct 18".
  • Both on rotations? See the days you're both home.
  • No Hitchstead account to make. Sharing runs through your own iCloud.
  • Partner on Android? Following in the app needs an iPhone. Send them a picture of the month or the year, or an ICS file for their calendar; those don't update by themselves.

Hitch or swing ran long? Add the holdover days and the rest of your rotation stays where it was.

On your Lock Screen, even with no signal.

Your countdown to the next travel day, or the countdown of someone you follow, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

Works offshore

Your saved schedule, countdown and widgets work without signal, on the rig, offshore or at camp. Sharing needs a connection; while you're offline, the people following you see the last schedule they received.

Private by design

No Hitchstead account to create, no ads, no tracking. Your schedule stays on your iPhone; private sharing uses your own iCloud.
