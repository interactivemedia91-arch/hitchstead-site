# hitchstead.com landing (index.html)

Scope: the marketing home page of hitchstead.com; /privacy/ and /support/ are Read pages in the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: rotation workers (oilfield, offshore, mining, FIFO) and their partners deciding whether Hitchstead fits; action = open the App Store page. Proof = real app screens (App Store frames in ~/Code/hitchstead/design/appstore/out), the real feature set, the real price. No ratings, reviews or user counts exist; none are invented.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS, GitHub Pages, one English page using both rotation and roster terms, no heavy JS, fast on phones with weak signal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the roster on the fridge: a whole year of a real 14/14 rotation, coloured day by day, is the first thing a visitor sees, and it proves the product before a word is read. It refuses the category default of a headline over a phone mockup and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: The app's Hard Hat system. Teal band gradient #1E7F72 to #10564C carrying cream #FFFCF4 type; cream ground #F7F3E8 and surface #FFFCF4; ink #20333F, secondary #52626B; the app's year colours for days (day #E6D3A6, night #B8C5E8, home #A6D9C7, travel #D4DE7C) and the orange change mark #EE7F33 for held-over days; New York / Georgia serif headlines, SF system text; rounded 28px sheets with a soft ink shadow.

STORY: In one glance: this is a calendar for rotation work that lays out every day of the year. Then: a holdover changes only its own days; the family follows free and is told when the homecoming moves; holidays and who is home; widgets; works without signal; private; free to start, $9.99 once. Then the visitor opens the App Store page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a teal band about 36% wide, full height: wordmark with icon, serif headline "Every day of your hitch, a year ahead.", one-line lede, the App Store badge as the primary action, price line under it. Right, a cream sheet titled "Mike's rotation · 2027" with twelve month grids in four columns, three October days marked as held over, and the legend; labelled as an example. On phones the band stacks above the sheet, which shows the coming months.

FORM: chosen structure: the year roster, 5th on my ordered list of 7, dealt as the lead. seed:3a98a2ebfe3df828. Signature interaction: tapping or hovering a day in the year shows what it is (Day shift, Night shift, Travel, Home, Held over) in a caption under the sheet, the way the app labels a day; the three held-over days settle in with one short colour change on load (reduced motion: shown at once).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
